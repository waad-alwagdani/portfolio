نظام رؤية حاسوبية للأدوية: يتعرّف على الحبة، ويعدّ الحبوب، ويقرأ علبة الدواء.

An end-to-end Python project: a fine-tuned CNN that identifies pills from photos, an OpenCV pipeline that counts them, and an OCR reader that extracts strength, batch and expiry from a medicine box. Deployed as an ONNX model inside a Streamlit app, with automated tests.

Identify a pill

MobileNetV3 pretrained on ImageNet, fine-tuned on 556 pill types (ePillID). Top-5 suggestions with confidence.

Count pills · Read a package

OpenCV: LAB segmentation → distance transform → watershed.
RapidOCR + rule-based parsing of strength, batch and expiry status.
