Delivery performance in global medicine supply chains

تحليل بيانات لأداء التسليم وتكلفة الشحن في سلاسل إمداد أدوية الإيدز والملاريا عالمياً.

10,324 real shipment lines from the USAID Supply Chain Management System: antiretrovirals and HIV/malaria test kits shipped to 43 countries between 2006 and 2015, worth $1.63 billion. Cleaned, analysed and turned into an interactive dashboard.

Key analyses

On-time rate by year: from 96–100% to 76.5% in 2011.
Cost per kg and punctuality by shipment mode.
When late, a median delay of 12 days.
Efavirenz/lamivudine/tenofovir price per pack, 2010–2015.

Findings

188.5% of lines arrived on time; reliability fell to 76.5% in 2011 and never fully recovered.
2Direct drop from the manufacturer was on time 94.7% vs. 82.8% through regional distribution centres.
3Air freight cost about 6× more per kg than ocean; trucks were cheapest but least punctual (76%).
4Recommendation: set safety stock from each lane's 90th-percentile delay, not the average.
